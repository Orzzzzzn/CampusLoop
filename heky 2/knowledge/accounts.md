# Student accounts and name privacy
CampusLoop uses student email verification and a vehicle description. Development accounts and on-screen verification codes are fictional local testing tools, not real enrollment verification. Student email verification does not verify driving eligibility.

Before a confirmed match, other students appear as stable Loop Member pseudonyms. Pending, waitlisted, declined, and canceled requests keep real names hidden. Your own profile shows your name to you. After confirmation, the owner and currently confirmed participants on that specific ride date can see one another's names. Other viewers still see pseudonyms. Confirmation on one recurring date does not reveal identities on every date. Canceling stops platform name disclosure for that date, but cannot erase names someone already saw.

The same name policy applies to ride records, applications, activity, chat, and RAG ride sources. Known registered names and emails in notes are masked for unmatched viewers. Personal details voluntarily written in public content can still identify someone. The database stores real account names. Do not infer identities from aliases.

The assistant reads ride posts and community guidance and cites sources. It cannot read private chat or authentication secrets and cannot book, accept, cancel, charge, or edit a ride. Use the app's action buttons. Some seeded accounts already have confirmed rides, so those matched names can be visible immediately.
