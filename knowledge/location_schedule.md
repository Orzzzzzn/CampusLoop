# Places, ranges, and date filters
Pickup and drop-off independently support Exact place or Nearby area. Search a place or address, select a result, and choose the allowed radius. The interface shows a place and range rather than editable coordinates. Old display-marker suffixes are removed from stored locations without changing coordinates or bookings. A location must have a resolved map point; typing a label alone does not resolve an address. A map click or browser-approved current location can also set that point. Pickup and drop-off start empty. Specific saved place names can be searched offline; broad region names are not automatically replaced with an arbitrary station. Other searches use a configurable Photon service and need internet access.

An exact place has zero flexibility radius. An area allows a radius from 0.1 to 10 miles around the chosen place. With both route filters active, pickup areas must overlap AND drop-off areas must overlap. Results are ranked by the sum of the center distances from the user's requested places. A single enabled location filter checks only that end. Suggested exact meeting points fall within both overlapping areas. The owner accepts the final proposed points when confirming a request.

Matching uses great-circle geographic distance and circle overlap with one metre of rounding tolerance. Radius is straight-line distance, not road distance, walking time, live traffic ETA, or computed driving detour. The listed detour limit is a separate preference for the driver to review. Check the actual meeting entrance before traveling.

The ordinary All rides board lists upcoming active offers and rider requests. Filter by role, Regular rides or One-time & round trips, an optional specific date, one departure hour, and selected pickup/drop-off areas. Local accounts browse the normal recurring and one-time ride sections; no scenario filter is used. My rides supports a specific date, Upcoming/Past/All scope and a departure-from filter. Departure until, advanced schedule filters and user-entered arrival deadlines are removed from the UI. Times use America/Los_Angeles and whole hours. Google travel minutes are an estimate, not an arrival promise.

Recurring schedules create separate ride dates with independent seats and cancellation. One-time trips create one date. A round trip uses one booking for both outbound and return legs on the same date, with the same party and capacity. Canceling that date cancels both legs. Other recurring dates stay unchanged.
