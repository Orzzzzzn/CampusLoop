# My rides, identity and date changes

My rides has You’re driving and Confirmed passenger tabs. Each contains Regular rides and One-time & round trips sections. Date and departure-hour filters combine with Upcoming, Past or All dates. Pending or waitlisted requests are managed under Requests, not Confirmed passenger. A rider post appears as a confirmed passenger ride only after accepting a driver. The actual driver's name, vehicle and driver rating are shown to confirmed passengers. Before confirmation, other students have anonymous aliases and a generic avatar. Confirmed partners see real names and real initials for their matched date only.

An owner can change departure and notes for one unrequested regular date. Other dates keep their schedule. A date with active requests must be skipped and reposted to get new agreement. Skip date affects one occurrence. Can’t make this week skips future occurrences of the same recurring ride in that date's Monday–Sunday week. An owner cancels the whole ride dates; a passenger cancels only their applications and may release seats to the waitlist. Later weeks are unchanged.

# Cost shares and recommendations

Automatic base price is Google Maps driving miles × $0.60 plus traffic-aware minutes × $0.30. Driver earnings are base × [1 + 0.65 × (n − 1)]; each rider pays earnings / n. Count confirmed passengers, including party members, but exclude the driver and pending/waitlisted requests. Posting shows Estimated Earnings from one rider to full seats; requesting shows Estimated Price from full seats to one rider. Acceptance and manual waitlist claims display current projected prices again. Confirmed prices update with passenger joins, cancellations and promotions. Round trips add Google distance and duration for each direction at its scheduled departure. Amounts are rounded to cents at the end, with no service fee or automatic-price cap. Per-person and earnings rounding can differ by a few cents when multiplied. CampusLoop does not process payments.

Every upcoming active date uses Google pricing, including older manually priced posts. Recurring dates each save a separate quote for their scheduled departure; an allowed date edit requests a fresh quote for that date only. Missing keys or unavailable/invalid Google route data hide manual amounts and block booking, without synthetic or straight-line pricing. New posts cannot enter a manual price. Original agreement amounts and historical dates stay stored. Requests and My rides show per-person and party totals; drivers also see current estimated earnings.

Recommended sorting weights route proximity 40, time proximity 25, price 15, role rating 10, available seats 10. Unselected locations/time and unrated members receive neutral scores. Driver posts favor lower cost share, rider posts favor higher budgets. Required filters are applied first. The score explains relevance, not acceptance probability or safety. Nearest, earliest and price sorts are available.

# Ratings and route pictures

Only actual drivers and their confirmed passengers can rate each other after the scheduled ride completion window (round trips after return departure plus one hour). Drivers rate riders, passengers rate the driver. A rating is 1–5 stars, one per partner per ride date. Editing updates the existing vote. Average and count are public below the avatar; identities stay private until confirmation. Past or All dates in My rides exposes rating actions for completed bookings.

Route thumbnails use OSRM road geometry between posted area centers, labelled OSRM map preview. Their duration excludes live traffic and is not used for pricing; Google pricing distance and traffic-aware time appear separately. When map routing fails, a dashed approximate connection is labelled as such. Online tiles and routing require internet. Exact agreed meeting points can differ from the posted centers.
